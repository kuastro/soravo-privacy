Soravo support

Soravo keeps the expiry dates of your medicine, vaccines and home items on your phone. There is no account and no server, so we cannot see or recover your data.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: kuastro.dev@gmail.com

Moving to a new phone

In Settings, open "Back up and transfer" and save a CSV file. On the new phone, open the same screen and restore from that file. It works between iPhone and Android.

Supporter purchase

If you bought "Become a supporter", use "Restore purchase" in Settings on a new phone signed in to the same store account.

Soravo gives no medical advice. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about doses and use.
